## Posting

The job description verbatim, if there is one. The agent mines it for the scorecard: the responsibilities, the must-have skills and the words the employer uses for them.

## Emphasis

## Scorecard

Filled by the agent from the posting: responsibilities, must-have skills, and the employer's own words for them.

## Fit

Filled by the agent before writing: what the facts support, what they do not, how large the gap is, and if the gap is large, the adjacent target on the same path the facts do support. The user's decision to proceed or redirect, dated.
